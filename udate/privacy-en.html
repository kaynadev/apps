Privacy Policy

uDate – Date Calculator (Android)

Version française

Last updated: November 14, 2026

In short
  • uDate does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data.
  • The app works completely offline and requests no permissions.
  • No ads, no usage analytics, no user account.

1. Who we are

The uDate app (package com.vasynet.datecalc) is published by Era Connect Solutions. For any question about this policy, contact us at kaynadev@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

None. uDate contains no analytics, tracking or advertising tools, and does not have permission to access the Internet. We therefore never receive any information about you or how you use the app.

3. Data stored on your device

So you don't have to re-enter everything, uDate stores the following only on your phone:

  • the dates and options entered in each tab (including the date of birth in the “Age” tab);
  • your countdown events (name and date);
  • your preferences (light / dark theme, last opened tab).

This data never leaves your device through the app. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data (Android Settings › Apps › uDate › Storage) or by uninstalling the app.

4. Android backup

If Android backup is turned on, the system may include these settings in your Google account backup so they can be restored on a new device. This backup is managed by Google according to your settings; we have no access to it. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.

5. Sharing with third parties

No data is shared with, sold to or transferred to third parties.

6. Permissions

uDate requests no Android permissions (no Internet, location, contacts or storage access).

7. Children

The app collects no data and can therefore be used by everyone, including children.

8. Your rights (GDPR)

Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete: you keep full control of it on your device. For any question, write to us at the address above. You may also lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country.

9. Changes

If this policy changes (for example when a feature is added), the new version will be published on this page with a new “last updated” date.
